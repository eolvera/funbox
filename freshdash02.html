EOlvera Portfolio Summary

This visualization aggregates MTD portfolio metrics (Sales, Credits, Chargebacks, Deposits, Returns) to provide a high-level view of portfolio health. In real-world scenarios, it helps identify anomalies (e.g., high chargeback spikes) for immediate investigation, enabling proactive risk mitigation.

Merchant Periodic Review Project Board

The project board visualizes merchant review progress across multiple metrics (KYC/AML, FICO, etc.) using a Gantt-like chart. In practice, it helps risk teams prioritize tasks, track compliance deadlines, and allocate resources efficiently by highlighting overdue or upcoming reviews.

Merchant Risk Scoring Model

A new scoring model calculates merchant risk based on five factors: flagged history, CNP/NP transaction percentage, settlement speed, chargeback ratio, and credit/refund ratio. Scores range from 0-100, with weights adjusted by merchant age.
